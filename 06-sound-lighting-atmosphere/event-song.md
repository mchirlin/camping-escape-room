# Event Song — "Blocks & Sunshine"

A second, companion song to the finale victory song (`victory-song.md`). Where
that one narrates the 10-craft adventure, this one is about the *feeling* of the
whole event — friendship and the "best day ever" rush — wrapped in the Minecraft
world (blocks, crafting, mining, biomes, mobs, day and night). It leans on the
vibe of the game, not the specific escape-room items and locations, with just a
sprinkle of specifics (a creeper, a diamond). Bright, glossy, radio-pop.

> Story source of truth: `../02-puzzle-design/flowchart.md`. This song is
> intentionally non-literal (no step-by-step crafts or real-world locations), so
> it stays evergreen even if game details shift — it just needs to stay true to
> the Minecraft spirit and the fun/teamwork of the day.

- **Audience:** kids ages 7–10, up to 6 players
- **Vibe:** feel-good modern radio pop — claps, big "oh-oh-oh" hooks,
  key-change final chorus — with a Minecraft heart
- **Length:** ~2–3 min
- **Format:** Suno-style (bracketed section/production tags, plain lyric lines)

## Style Prompt (for Suno / Udio / etc.)

```
Bright, glossy modern feel-good radio pop. Punchy four-on-the-floor beat, hand-claps and finger-snaps, shimmering synths,
bouncy plucked bass, and a massive, sticky singalong chorus with "oh-oh-oh" hooks. Add
subtle 8-bit / chiptune sparkles as a playful nod to Minecraft. Sunny major key, polished
and euphoric, adventurous "best day ever" feeling. Layered stacked vocals, a stripped-back
bridge that builds into a key-change final chorus. Clean, youthful lead vocal, super
kid-friendly and easy to sing. Upbeat, around 118–124 BPM.
```
