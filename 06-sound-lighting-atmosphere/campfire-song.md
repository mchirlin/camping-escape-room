# Campfire Song — "Embers"

A gentle wind-down song to close the set, alongside the finale victory song
(`victory-song.md`) and the upbeat event song (`event-song.md`). Slow, cozy,
acoustic singalong meant for dusk around a real campfire. Same Minecraft-tinged
world, softer and more heartfelt.

> Story source of truth: `../02-puzzle-design/flowchart.md`. Intentionally
> non-literal (no step-by-step crafts or real-world locations), so it stays
> evergreen — it just needs to stay true to the spirit of the day: adventure,
> teamwork, and friendship.

- **Audience:** kids ages 7–10, up to 6 players
- **Vibe:** warm acoustic campfire folk-pop — fingerpicked guitar / ukulele,
  easy group "la-la-la" singalong, tender end-of-day glow
- **Length:** ~2–3 min
- **Format:** Suno-style (bracketed section/production tags, plain lyric lines)

---

## Lyrics (Suno format)

```
[Intro - soft fingerpicked guitar and ukulele]

[Verse 1 - single voice, gentle]
The sun's goin' down on our blocky little world,
Stars startin' up like a torch in the dark.
We ran and we crafted, we mined and we laughed,
And now we're just here with the fire and our hearts.

[Chorus - warm group singalong]
So gather 'round, gather 'round the glow,
Sing it slow, let the whole world know,
We built somethin' good, somethin' brave and true,
Ohhh... me and you.
La-la-la, la-la-la...

[Verse 2]
We started with nothin' but a page and a dream,
Now look at the friends that we found on the way.
No creepers tonight, just the crackle and the light,
And a story we'll carry from this day.

[Chorus - warm group singalong]
So gather 'round, gather 'round the glow,
Sing it slow, let the whole world know,
We built somethin' good, somethin' brave and true,
Ohhh... me and you.
La-la-la, la-la-la...

[Bridge - quietest moment, almost spoken, then swell]
The blocks may fade, the day may end,
But you don't lose a world when you've made a friend.
When it's dark again and you don't know where to go,
Just gather 'round the glow.

[Final Chorus - everyone, swaying]
So gather 'round, gather 'round the glow,
Sing it slow, let the whole world know,
We built somethin' good, somethin' brave and true,
Ohhh... me and you.
We built somethin' good that'll never fade away,
Ohhh... this perfect day.

[Outro - soft and warm]
La-la-la, la-la-la... gather 'round the glow.
```

## Style Prompt (for Suno / Udio / etc.)

```
Warm, gentle acoustic campfire folk-pop singalong. Soft fingerpicked acoustic guitar
and ukulele, light hand percussion, a cozy sway feel. Intimate solo lead vocal in the
verses opening into a warm, easy group singalong on the chorus with "la-la-la" hooks.
Nostalgic, heartfelt, end-of-the-day glow — think a friendly campfire lullaby, not a
big production. Simple, singable, kid-friendly. Slow and tender, around 72–80 BPM.
Ends soft and warm.
```
