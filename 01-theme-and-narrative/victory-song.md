# Victory Song — "Craft Your Way Home"

Plays at the very end of the event, right after the players blow open the
treasure chest and find the Ender Dragon egg. Triumphant, credits-rolling
finale.

> Story source of truth: `../02-puzzle-design/flowchart.md`. Lyrics follow the
> 10-craft arc (diary → crafting table → compass → pickaxe → map → fishing pole
> → diamond pickaxe → torch → sword → spyglass → TNT → treasure).

- **Audience:** kids ages 7–10, up to 6 players
- **Vibe:** anthemic stadium pop-rock — stomp-clap anthem, big chantable
  "whoa-oh" hooks, quiet-loud dynamics, beat-drop chorus
- **Length:** ~2–3 min
- **Format:** Suno-style (bracketed section/production tags, plain lyric lines)

---

## Style Prompt (for Suno / Udio / etc.)

```
Anthemic alt-pop / stadium pop-rock. Big stomp-clap
percussion, thick tribal floor-toms, punchy staccato verses that build tension, and
a huge singalong "whoa-oh-oh" gang-vocal chorus with a hard beat drop. Quiet-loud
dynamics: sparse, whispery bridge that explodes into the final chorus. Driving bass,
electronic pulse under organic drums, triumphant and heroic. Clean confident lead
vocal, kid-friendly and easy to chant. Major key, roughly 100–110 BPM. Family-friendly
adventure anthem, credits-rolling energy.
```
